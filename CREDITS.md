# Pixeltopia — Credits

## Art
- **Tile atlas** (`assets/sprites/tiles.png`): original pixel art made for Pixeltopia.
- **Parallax backgrounds** (`assets/backgrounds/*.png`): original, generated by
  `tools/art/gen_bg.py` (procedural, seamless layers for the 4 world themes:
  Meadow, Forest, Desert, Night).

## Music & sound
- **11 music tracks** (`assets/audio/music/*.ogg`) and **26 sound effects**
  (`assets/audio/sfx/*.ogg`): original, synthesized from code by
  `tools/audio/gen_audio.py` (numpy + ffmpeg/libvorbis). No samples or third-party
  recordings are used.
